WhatsApp Business Product Catalogs

Create professional digital product catalogs with photographs, descriptions and pricing, and communicate directly with customers through WhatsApp Business.

1. Introduction

WhatsApp Business Product Catalogs provide Self-Help Groups (SHGs), artisans, small businesses and entrepreneurs with a simple digital method to showcase their products directly to customers.

Businesses can add product photographs, names, descriptions, prices and other relevant information to create an organized digital catalog. Customers can browse the available products and communicate with the business through WhatsApp.

Objective: Enable SHGs and small businesses to create an accessible digital product showcase, respond efficiently to customer enquiries and support direct order communication through WhatsApp Business.

3. Creating a Product Catalog

A well-organized catalog should provide customers with clear and accurate information about each product.

  • Clear and relevant product photograph
  • Product name
  • Short and informative product description
  • Current product price
  • Available sizes, variants or options, where applicable
  • Product availability information
  • Relevant delivery or collection information

4. Recommended Catalog Setup Process

  1. Create or configure a WhatsApp Business account.
  2. Complete the business profile with accurate business information.
  3. Prepare clear photographs of the products.
  4. Add products to the catalog.
  5. Enter product names, descriptions and current prices.
  6. Organize products into appropriate categories where available.
  7. Review product information for accuracy.
  8. Prepare quick replies for frequently asked questions.
  9. Share relevant catalog items with interested customers.
  10. Confirm availability, quantity, delivery details and final pricing before accepting an order.

5. Automated Quick Replies

Quick replies can help businesses respond efficiently to frequently asked customer questions. Common responses can be prepared in advance and used when appropriate.

Customer Enquiry Thank you for your enquiry. Please tell us which product and quantity you require.
Product Availability This product is currently available. We can confirm the final order details in this chat.
Delivery Information Please share your delivery location so we can provide the applicable delivery information.
Order Confirmation Thank you for your order. We will confirm product availability and the final order details shortly.

7. Order Management Practices

Businesses should establish a clear process for handling customer orders received through WhatsApp.

  • Confirm the exact product and quantity requested.
  • Check product availability before confirming the order.
  • Communicate the final price clearly.
  • Confirm delivery or collection arrangements.
  • Maintain appropriate records of confirmed orders.
  • Provide customers with relevant order updates.
  • Update the catalog when products become unavailable or prices change.

8. Security & Customer Protection

Important Security Practices
  • Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs or other confidential credentials with customers or strangers.
  • Do not treat payment screenshots or messages as proof of payment. Verify the actual transaction through the relevant payment system.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious payment or login links sent by unknown users.
  • Verify unusual customer requests before processing orders or refunds.
  • Keep business account access restricted to authorised persons.
  • Maintain accurate product and pricing information to avoid customer disputes.

9. Best Practices for SHGs & Small Businesses

  • Use clear, well-lit and relevant product photographs.
  • Keep product descriptions short and informative.
  • Display accurate and current pricing.
  • Update product availability regularly.
  • Respond to customer enquiries professionally.
  • Use quick replies for common questions.
  • Confirm order details before accepting an order.
  • Keep appropriate records of customer orders.
  • Communicate delivery conditions clearly.
  • Review account security settings regularly.

11. Conclusion

WhatsApp Business Product Catalogs can help Self-Help Groups, artisans and small businesses create a professional digital product showcase while maintaining direct communication with customers.

By combining clear product photographs, accurate pricing, informative descriptions and quick replies, businesses can make customer communication more organized and efficient.

A well-maintained WhatsApp catalog can support digital visibility, customer engagement and more structured order management for growing businesses.
