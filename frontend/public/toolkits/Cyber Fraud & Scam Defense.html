Digital Safety & Cyber Awareness

Cyber Fraud & Scam Defense

Learn how to identify fake customer payment screenshots, phishing SMS links, fraudulent messages and unauthorized account access attempts while protecting your business, customers and digital payments.

Introduction

Building awareness is the first step towards safer digital business.

Digital payments, online marketplaces and messaging applications have made it easier for small businesses, Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and entrepreneurs to communicate with customers and receive orders. However, increased digital activity can also expose businesses to phishing, fake payment confirmations, impersonation and account takeover attempts.

Cyber Fraud & Scam Defense focuses on practical habits that help users verify payments, identify suspicious links, protect account credentials and respond quickly when something appears unusual.

Important Rule: Verify Before You Trust

Never consider a payment complete merely because a customer shows a screenshot or sends an SMS claiming that money has been transferred. Always verify the transaction through the official payment application, bank account or merchant dashboard before providing goods or services.

Common Cyber Fraud Risks

Recognize the warning signs before a scam causes financial or account-related loss.

💳

Fake Payment Screenshots

Fraudsters may show edited or fabricated payment screenshots to convince a seller that a transaction has been completed.

🔗

Phishing Links

Fraudulent SMS, emails or messages may contain links designed to trick users into entering passwords, banking information or other sensitive details.

📱

Fake Customer Support

Scammers may pretend to be bank, payment service or marketplace representatives and ask for confidential information or remote access.

👤

Impersonation

Criminals can impersonate customers, suppliers, employees or business contacts to create false payment or order requests.

🔐

Account Takeover

Weak passwords, reused credentials and exposed verification codes can increase the risk of unauthorized account access.

⚠️

Urgent Requests

Pressure tactics such as "pay immediately" or "verify your account now" are common warning signs of suspicious communication.

How to Identify Fake Payment Screenshots

A screenshot is not proof that money has reached your account.

Warning Sign What to Check
Payment screenshot only Check your own bank or payment application instead of relying on the screenshot.
Transaction ID appears suspicious Verify the transaction reference through the official payment platform.
Customer creates urgency Take time to confirm the actual payment status.
Amount does not match Compare the amount received with the order amount.
SMS claims payment received Do not depend only on an SMS. Check the official account or merchant dashboard.
Customer asks for OTP or PIN Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or authentication codes.

Example: Suspicious Payment Proof

⚠ Payment Screenshot

₹2,500

Customer says: "Payment completed. Please deliver the product."


Do not treat this screenshot as final proof of payment.

Correct Action: Open your official bank/payment application and verify whether ₹2,500 has actually been credited before completing the order.

Phishing SMS & Suspicious Links

Be cautious when a message asks you to click a link, provide information or take immediate action.

Example of a suspicious message:

"Your merchant account requires immediate verification. Click the link below to avoid suspension."

example-suspicious-link.com/verify

Warning: Do not open suspicious links.
🔍

Check the Sender

Examine the sender carefully. A familiar-looking name does not necessarily mean the message is genuine.

🔗

Inspect the Link

Be cautious with unfamiliar or shortened links. When in doubt, open the official website or application directly instead of using the message link.

🚨

Watch for Urgency

Messages demanding immediate action, threatening account closure or promising rewards should be treated carefully.

Never Share Confidential Information

Legitimate support staff should not require you to disclose your passwords, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, OTP or authentication codes. Do not enter such information after following an unexpected message link.

Prevent Unauthorized Account Access

Strong account security reduces the risk of unauthorized access to business and payment-related accounts.

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords for important accounts and avoid using the same password everywhere.

🛡️ Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Where available, enable two-factor authentication to add another layer of protection.

📲 Protect Your Phone

Use a screen lock and keep your operating system and important applications updated.

🚫 Never Share OTPs

Verification codes are confidential. Never share them with customers, strangers or people claiming to provide technical support.

💻 Review Logged-In Devices

Periodically check account security settings for unfamiliar devices or sessions and sign out of sessions you do not recognize.

📧 Secure Business Email

Protect the email account associated with business services because it may be used for password recovery.

Safe Digital Payment Practices

Follow a simple verification process for every customer transaction.

1

Receive Order

Confirm the customer's order and the exact amount.

2

Check Payment

Verify the payment directly through your official payment or bank application.

3

Confirm Credit

Make sure the actual amount has been credited before processing the order.

4

Complete Order

Only after successful verification should the product or service be delivered.

Things You Should Never Do

Simple rules that can prevent many common digital payment scams.

✕ Do not accept a screenshot as the only payment proof.
✕ Do not share your UPI PIN with anyone.
✕ Do not share OTPs or verification codes.
✕ Do not click unknown payment or account links.
✕ Do not install apps at the request of an unknown caller.
✕ Do not allow unnecessary remote access to your device.
✕ Do not rush because someone creates artificial urgency.
✓ Always verify important transactions independently.

What to Do If You Suspect Fraud

Quick and careful action can help limit further risk.

  1. Stop the transaction: Do not send additional money or information.
  2. Secure your accounts: Change compromised passwords and review account security settings.
  3. Contact the relevant service: Use the official bank, payment provider or marketplace support channel.
  4. Preserve evidence: Keep relevant transaction details, messages and communication records without forwarding sensitive information unnecessarily.
  5. Report the incident: If financial fraud or unauthorized access has occurred, report it promptly through the appropriate official cybercrime or financial-fraud reporting channel.
  6. Inform your group: For SHGs or small business teams, alert relevant members so that the same scam does not affect others.

Cyber Safety Implementation Checklist

A simple checklist for SHGs, entrepreneurs and small businesses.

Security Area Recommended Practice
Payments Verify every payment through the official account or merchant application.
Passwords Use strong and unique passwords.
OTP Keep OTPs and authentication codes private.
UPI PIN Never share the UPI PIN with another person.
SMS Links Avoid unexpected links and access services through official applications or websites.
Devices Keep devices and applications updated and protected with a screen lock.
Account Review Regularly check account activity and unfamiliar login sessions.

Conclusion

Cyber safety is an important part of running a modern digital business. Fake payment screenshots, phishing links, impersonation and unauthorized account access can create serious risks for entrepreneurs and Self-Help Groups.

By verifying payments independently, protecting confidential information, using strong account security and responding quickly to suspicious activity, businesses can reduce their exposure to common digital scams.

Think before you click. Verify before you trust.
