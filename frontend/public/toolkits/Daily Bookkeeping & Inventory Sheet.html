Digital Financial Management

Daily Bookkeeping & Inventory Sheet

Simple digital templates to record daily purchases, sales, inventory, expenses, profits and group dividend shares in an organized manner.

1. Introduction

Maintaining accurate financial and inventory records is essential for Self-Help Groups (SHGs), small businesses, artisans and entrepreneurs.

A daily bookkeeping and inventory sheet provides a structured method for recording raw material purchases, product sales, business expenses, stock levels and profits.

Objective: Help SHGs and small businesses maintain simple, consistent and transparent financial records using digital spreadsheets or mobile ledger tools.

2. Key Benefits

01

Maintain a regular record of purchases, sales, expenses and other business transactions.

02

05

9. Best Practices

  • Record transactions as soon as reasonably possible.
  • Keep purchase bills, invoices and relevant receipts.
  • Update inventory after significant stock movements.
  • Keep sales and expense entries separate.
  • Review records regularly for errors or duplicate entries.
  • Restrict editing access to authorised group members.
  • Keep digital records backed up appropriately.
  • Reconcile recorded payments with actual bank or cash balances regularly.
  • Keep member-share or surplus-allocation records transparent and properly documented.

10. Implementation Checklist

  • Select a bookkeeping method.
  • Create a daily transaction sheet.
  • Create an inventory tracking sheet.
  • Record raw material purchases.
  • Record daily sales.
  • Record business expenses.
  • Update stock levels.
  • Review daily and monthly totals.
  • Maintain approved group share or surplus records.
  • Back up important records regularly.

11. Conclusion

Daily bookkeeping and inventory management provide SHGs and small businesses with a structured way to monitor their financial and operational activities.

Using simple spreadsheet or mobile ledger templates can make it easier to record purchases, sales, expenses, inventory movements and group-level financial information.

Consistent record keeping improves transparency, supports better business decisions and provides a stronger foundation for sustainable digital business management.
