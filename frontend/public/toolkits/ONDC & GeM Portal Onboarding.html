Digital Business Enablement

ONDC & GeM Portal Onboarding

A professional guide for Self-Help Groups, small businesses and entrepreneurs to access digital commerce opportunities and government procurement through ONDC and GeM.

1. Introduction

Digital commerce and government procurement platforms provide Self-Help Groups (SHGs), artisans, small businesses and entrepreneurs with opportunities to expand their market reach and connect with institutional buyers.

The Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC) enables participation in an interoperable digital commerce network, while the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) provides an online platform for government procurement.

Objective: Enable eligible SHGs and businesses to establish a verified digital presence, understand onboarding requirements and explore new selling and procurement opportunities through ONDC and GeM.

2. Understanding ONDC

The Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC) is an open network designed to enable buyers and sellers to interact across participating digital commerce applications.

ONDC follows an interoperable network approach, allowing participating sellers to potentially reach customers through different buyer applications and network participants.

Key Benefits of ONDC

01
Wider Market Access

Eligible sellers can potentially reach customers beyond their immediate local market through participating network applications.

02
Open Network Model

ONDC is designed around interoperability between participating network members rather than dependence on a single marketplace.

03
Digital Visibility

Businesses can establish a digital presence and showcase their products or services to potential customers.

04
Business Opportunities

Digital commerce can provide additional market opportunities for SHGs, artisans and small businesses.

3. ONDC Seller Onboarding Process

ONDC seller onboarding is generally completed through a participating seller-side application or network participant.

  1. Identify the products or services that the SHG or business intends to offer.
  2. Select an appropriate ONDC seller-side participant or seller application.
  3. Complete the required seller and business information.
  4. Provide the required bank and business details for verification.
  5. Add product or service information, including descriptions, prices and other required details.
  6. Configure order fulfilment, delivery and customer support arrangements where applicable.
  7. Complete the required onboarding and verification process.
Important: ONDC onboarding requirements, services, charges, settlement arrangements and other conditions may vary between participating network participants. Always review the current terms before registration.

4. Understanding GeM

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is an online public procurement platform used by government departments, organisations and public-sector entities to procure eligible goods and services from registered sellers and service providers.

For eligible SHGs and small enterprises, GeM can provide an opportunity to participate in government procurement and connect with institutional buyers.

Key Benefits of GeM

01
Government Procurement Access

Registered sellers can participate in government procurement opportunities available through the platform.

02
Digital Procurement

Product listings, procurement activities and related processes can be managed through a centralized digital marketplace.

03
Business Visibility

Eligible sellers can showcase their products and services to government buyers.

04
Institutional Market

GeM provides access to procurement opportunities from participating government organisations and departments.

5. GeM Seller Onboarding Process

Eligible businesses and organisations can register on the Government e-Marketplace by following the applicable seller registration process.

  1. Access the official Government e-Marketplace platform.
  2. Select the appropriate seller or service-provider registration option.
  3. Create the seller account using authorised business information.
  4. Provide the required organisation, identity, bank and tax-related information applicable to the seller category.
  5. Complete the required verification procedures.
  6. Create and complete the seller profile.
  7. Add eligible products or services with accurate descriptions and specifications.
  8. Review applicable pricing, delivery and procurement requirements.
  9. Monitor relevant government procurement opportunities and participate where eligible.

6. ONDC vs GeM

Although both platforms support digital business opportunities, their primary purposes are different.

Feature ONDC GeM
Primary Purpose Open digital commerce network Government procurement marketplace
Target Market Consumers and businesses through participating network participants Government departments and eligible institutional buyers
Business Opportunity Digital commerce and wider customer reach Government procurement opportunities
Seller Model Participation through network participants Seller registration on GeM
Charges Depend on applicable participant and service terms Depend on applicable GeM rules and requirements

7. Information and Documents to Prepare

The exact documentation requirements may vary depending on the platform, organisation structure, seller category and services being offered.

  • SHG or organisation registration information
  • Authorised representative details
  • Bank account information
  • Tax-related information, where applicable
  • Business and product information
  • Product photographs and descriptions
  • Pricing and inventory information
  • Business contact and address information

9. Security & Compliance Guidelines

Important Security Practices
  • Use official government websites and authorised platform channels for registration.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs or other confidential credentials with third parties.
  • Verify registration-related communications before sharing documents or making payments.
  • Check applicable fees, commissions, logistics charges and service costs before accepting a commercial arrangement.
  • Maintain accurate product, pricing and business information.
  • Keep account credentials secure and use available security features.

10. Understanding Platform Charges

Digital commerce platforms can help businesses reduce certain traditional distribution barriers and provide access to wider markets. However, businesses should not assume that every service is completely free or carries zero commission.

Applicable commissions, subscription fees, logistics charges, payment-related costs or other expenses may depend on the platform, participating organisation, seller category and services selected.

Best Practice: Always review the latest pricing, commission structure, service conditions and settlement terms before completing registration or accepting an offer.

11. Implementation Checklist

  • Identify products and services suitable for digital selling or government procurement.
  • Verify SHG/business eligibility.
  • Prepare business and banking information.
  • Prepare product photographs and descriptions.
  • Select the appropriate ONDC seller-side participant, where applicable.
  • Complete GeM registration where eligible.
  • Complete identity and business verification.
  • Add accurate product and service information.
  • Review applicable fees and commercial terms.
  • Monitor orders, procurement opportunities and customer requirements.

12. Conclusion

ONDC and GeM provide important digital opportunities for eligible Self-Help Groups, small businesses, artisans and entrepreneurs.

ONDC can help businesses participate in India's evolving open digital commerce ecosystem, while GeM can provide access to government procurement opportunities.

Successful onboarding requires accurate business information, appropriate documentation, product readiness and awareness of applicable platform requirements.

By developing a strong digital presence and using the appropriate commerce and procurement channels, SHGs can expand their market reach and explore new opportunities for sustainable business growth.
